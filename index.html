About us

Who we are

With a legacy spanning two decades, Choice Property Finance is a family-run mortgage brokerage that embodies trust, expertise and personalised financial solutions.

Since our establishment we have been dedicated to helping individuals and businesses achieve homeownership, growth and investment success. We specialise in self-employed professionals, first-home buyers, and commercial ventures seeking expansion finance.

We don’t just secure mortgages — we build bridges to brighter financial futures. Locally based and proudly serving the Northern Beaches of NSW.
